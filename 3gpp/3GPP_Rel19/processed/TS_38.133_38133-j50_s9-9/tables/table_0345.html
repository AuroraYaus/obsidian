Configuration TL1-RSRP_Measurement_Period_CSI-RS_RedCap (ms)
non-DRX max(TReport, ceil(M*P)*TCSI-RS)
DRX cycle ≤ 320 ms max(TReport, ceil(1.5*M*P)*max(TDRX,TCSI-RS))
DRX cycle > 320 ms ceil(M*P)*TDRX
NOTE 1:TCSI-RS is the periodicity of CSI-RS configured for L1-RSRP measurement. TDRX is the DRX cycle length. TReport is configured periodicity for reporting.NOTE 2:the requirements are applicable provided that the CSI-RS resource configured for L1-RSRP measurement is transmitted with Density = 3.
