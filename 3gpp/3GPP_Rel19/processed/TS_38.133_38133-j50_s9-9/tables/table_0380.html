Configuration TL1-RSRP_Measurement_Period_SSB_CDP (ms)
non-DRX max(TReport, ceil(M*P)*TSSB_CDP)
DRX cycle ≤ 320 ms max(TReport, ceil(K *M*P)*max(TDRX,TSSB_CDP))
