NOTE 1:For a UE supporting concurrent GAPs, if multiple concurrent GAPs are configured, the MGRP is the periodicity of the activated Pre-MG or the MG pattern associated to the intra-frequency layer.NOTE 2: KFR is a scaling factor depending on the frequency range and the SSB SCS. For FR2-1, KFR = 1. For FR2-2: KFR = 1 if the SCS of the SSB of the cell being detected is 120 kHz, KFR = 2 if the SCS of the SSB of the cell being detected is 480 kHz, and KFR = 3 if the SCS of the SSB of the cell being detected is 960 kHz.
