MCGFailureInformation field descriptions
measResultFreqListEUTRAThe field contains available results of measurements on EUTRA frequencies the UE is configured to measure by measConfig.
measResultFreqListGERANThe field contains available results of measurements on GERAN frequencies the UE is configured to measure by measConfig.
measResultFreqListNRThe field contains available results of measurements on NR frequencies the UE is configured to measure by measConfig.
measResultFreqListUTRAThe field contains available results of measurements on UTRA frequencies the UE is configured to measure by measConfig.
measResultSCGIncludes the NR MeasResultSCG-Failure IE as specified in TS 38.331 [82]. The field contains available results of measurements on NR frequencies the UE is configured to measure by the NR RRCConfiguration message.
