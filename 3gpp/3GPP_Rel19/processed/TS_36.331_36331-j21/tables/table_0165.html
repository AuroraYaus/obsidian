Conditional presence Explanation
Bitmap1 The field is optionally present, need OR, if value of slotBitmap corresponding to at least one subframe is '01'; otherwise the field is not present.
Bitmap2 The field is optionally present, need OR, if value of slotBitmap corresponding to at least one subframe is '10'; otherwise the field is not present.
FDDandTDDnoDL The field is mandatory present for TDD when resource reservation for DL is not configured, and for FDD; otherwise the field is optionally present, need OP.
