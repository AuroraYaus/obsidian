SL-V2X-PacketDuplicationConfig field descriptions
allowedCarrierFreqList, allowedCarrierFreqSet1, allowedCarrierFreqSet2Indicates, for V2X sidelink communication, the set of carrier frequencies applicable for the transmission of the MAC SDUs from the sidelink logical channels whose associated destination are included in destinationInfoList (see TS 36.321 [6]). If present, E-UTRAN shall ensure allowedCarrierFreqSet1 and allowedCarrierFreqSet2 do not include the same carrier frequency.
threshSL-ReliabilityIndicates the reliability threshold used to determine whether sidelinik packet duplication is configured and activated for V2X sidelink communication transmission. See TS 36.323 [8] and TS 36.321 [6].
