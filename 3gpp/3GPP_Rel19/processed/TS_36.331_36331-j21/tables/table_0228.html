LogMeasResultListWLAN field descriptions
rssiWLANMeasured WLAN RSSI result in dBm.
rtt-WLANThis field provides the measured round trip time between the target device and WLAN AP and optionally the accuracy expressed as the standard deviation of the delay. Units for each of these are 1000ns, 100ns, 10ns, 1ns, and 0.1ns as defined in TS 36.355 [54].
wlan-IdentifiersIndicates the WLAN parameters used for identification of the WLAN for which the measurement results are applicable.
