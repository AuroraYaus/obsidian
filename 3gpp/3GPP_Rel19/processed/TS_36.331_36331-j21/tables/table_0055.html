ULDedicatedMessageSegment field descriptions
segmentNumberIdentifies the sequence number of a segment within the encoded UL DCCH message.
rrc-MessageSegmentContainerIncludes a segment of the encoded UL DCCH message. The size of the included segment in this container should be small enough that the resulting encoded RRC message PDU is less than or equal to the PDCP SDU size limit.
rrc-MessageSegmentTypeIndicates whether the included UL DCCH message segment is the last segment or not.
