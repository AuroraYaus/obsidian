ULInformationTransferMRDC field descriptions
ul-DCCH-MessageNRIncludes the UL-DCCH-Message as defined in TS 38.331 [82]. In this version of the specification, the field is only used to transfer the NR RRC MeasurementReport, NR RRC UEAssistanceInformation, NR RRC IABOtherInformation, NR RRC FailureInformation, and the NR RRC RRCReconfigurationComplete messages.
