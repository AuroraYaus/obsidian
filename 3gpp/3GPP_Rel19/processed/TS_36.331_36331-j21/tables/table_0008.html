HandoverFromEUTRAPreparationRequest field descriptions
concurrPrepCDMA2000-HRPDValue TRUE indicates that upper layers should initiate concurrent preparation for handover to CDMA2000 HRPD in addition to preparation for enhanced CS fallback to CDMA2000 1xRTT.
dualRxTxRedirectIndicatorValue TRUE indicates that the second radio of the dual Rx/Tx UE is being redirected to CDMA2000 1xRTT, as specified in TS 23.272 [51].
redirectCarrierCDMA2000-1XRTTUsed to indicate the CDMA2000 1xRTT carrier frequency where the UE is being redirected to.
