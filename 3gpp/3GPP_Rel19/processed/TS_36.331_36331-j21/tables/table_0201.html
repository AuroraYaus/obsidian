ConditionalReconfiguration field descriptions
attemptCondReconfIf present, the UE shall perform conditional reconfiguration if selected cell is a target candidate cell and it is the first cell selection after failure as described in 5.3.7.3.
condReconfigurationToAddModListList of conditional reconfigurations (i.e. conditional handover, conditional PSCell addition or inter-SN conditional PSCell change) to add and/or modify.
condReconfigurationToRemoveListList of conditional reconfigurations (i.e. conditional handover, conditional PSCell addition or inter-SN conditional PSCell change) to remove.
