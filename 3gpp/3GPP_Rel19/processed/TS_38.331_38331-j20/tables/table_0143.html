SIB25 field descriptions
coverageAreaInfoListContains a list of TN coverage area's information to assist skipping TN measurements for NTN UEs in RRC_IDLE and RRC_INACTIVE, as defined in TS 38.304 [20].
tn-DistanceRadiusDistance from the TN coverage area reference location. It is used for skipping TN measurements in RRC_IDLE and RRC_INACTIVE, as defined in TS 38.304 [20]. Each step represents 1m.
