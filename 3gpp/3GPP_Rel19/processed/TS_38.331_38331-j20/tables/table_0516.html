Conditional Presence Explanation
CellLevel The field is mandatory present if the RateMatchPattern is defined on cell level. The field is absent when the RateMatchPattern is defined on BWP level or defined for MBS broadcast CFR. If the RateMatchPattern is defined on BWP level, the UE applies the SCS of the BWP and if RateMatchPattern is defined for MBS broadcast CFR, the UE applies the SCS of the initial BWP or RedCap-specific initial BWP (if configured) for (e)RedCap UEs.
