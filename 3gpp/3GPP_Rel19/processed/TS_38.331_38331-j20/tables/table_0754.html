SL-RelayUE-Config field descriptions
threshHighRelayIndicates the upper threshold of Uu RSRP for a UE that is in network coverage to evaluate AS layer conditions for U2N relay UE or Last U2N Relay UE operation.
threshLowRelayIndicates the lower threshold of Uu RSRP for a UE that is in network coverage to evaluate AS layer conditions for U2N relay UE or Last U2N Relay UE operation.
