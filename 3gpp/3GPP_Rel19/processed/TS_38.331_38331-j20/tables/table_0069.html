SCGFailureInformation field descriptions
measResultFreqListThe field contains available results of measurements on NR frequencies the UE is configured to measure by measConfig.
measResultSCG-FailureThe field contains the MeasResultSCG-Failure IE which includes available results of measurements on NR frequencies the UE is configured to measure by the NR SCG RRCReconfiguration message.
previousPSCellIdThis field indicates the physical cell id and carrier frequency of the cell that is the source PSCell of the last PSCell change or conditional PSCell change. In case of PSCell addition or conditional PSCell addition or subsequent CPA, this field is absent. In case of subsequent CPC, this field indicates the physical cell identity and carrier frequency of the source PSCell associated to the last executed CPC.
failedPSCellIdThis field indicates the physical cell id and carrier frequency of:-the cell in which SCG failure is detected; or-the target PSCell of the failed PSCell change or addition; or--the target PSCell of the failed conditional PSCell change or addition; or-the target PSCell of the failed subsequent CPAC.
timeSCGFailureThis field is used to indicate the time elapsed since the last execution of RRCReconfiguration with reconfigurationWithSync for the SCG until the SCG failure. Actual value = field value * 100ms. The maximum value 1023 means 102.3s or longer.
