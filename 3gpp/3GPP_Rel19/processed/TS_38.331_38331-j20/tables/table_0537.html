CellIndividualOffsetList field descriptions
cellIndividualOffsetCell individual offsets applicable to a specific measurement event. If this field is present, the UE, for the same cell, shall ignore the cell individual offset configured within the MeasObjectNR of the measID associated with this ReportConfigNR.
physCellIdPhysical cell identity of a cell in the cell list.
ssbFrequencyIndicates the NR frequency of SS for which cellIndividualOffset is applicable. If the field is not configured, the NR frequency of SS indicated by ssbFrequency indicated within the MeasObjectNR of the measID associated with this ReportConfigNR applies.
