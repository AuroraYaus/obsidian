DAPS-UplinkPowerConfig field descriptions
p-DAPS-SourceThe maximum total transmit power to be used by the UE in the source cell group during DAPS handover.
p-DAPS-TargetThe maximum total transmit power to be used by the UE in the target cell group during DAPS handover.
uplinkPowerSharingDAPS-ModeIndicates the uplink power sharing mode that the UE uses in DAPS handover (see TS 38.213 [13]).
