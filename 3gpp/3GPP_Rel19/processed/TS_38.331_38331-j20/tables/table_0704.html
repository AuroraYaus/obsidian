SL-BWP-PoolConfigCommon field descriptions
sl-TxPoolExceptionalIndicates the resources by which the UE is allowed to perform NR sidelink transmission in exceptional conditions on the configured BWP. For the PSFCH related configuration, if configured, will be used for PSFCH transmission/reception. This field is not present when SL-BWP-PoolConfigCommon is included in SidelinkPreconfigNR.
