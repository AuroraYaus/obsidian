SL-PeriodicalReportConfig field descriptions
sl-ReportAmountNumber of sidelink measurement reports applicable for sl-Periodical report type.
sl-ReportIntervalIndicates the interval between periodical reports (i.e., when sl-ReportAmount exceeds 1) for sl-Periodical report type.
sl-ReportQuantityThe sidelink measurement quantities to be included in the sidelink measurement report.
