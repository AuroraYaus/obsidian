VarShortMAC-Input field descriptions
targetCellIdentityAn input variable used to calculate the shortMAC-I. Set to the cellIdentity of the first PLMN-Identity in the PLMN-IdentityInfoList broadcasted in SIB1 of the target cell i.e. the cell the UE is trying to reestablish the connection.
source-c-RNTISet to C-RNTI that the UE had in the PCell it was connected to prior to the reestablishment.
sourcePhysCellIdSet to the physical cell identity of the PCell the UE was connected to prior to the reestablishment.
