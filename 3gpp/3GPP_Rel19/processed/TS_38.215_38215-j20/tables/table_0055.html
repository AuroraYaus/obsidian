Definition Square Generalized Cosine Similarity (SGCS) value is defined in Clause 5.2.1.4.6 of [6, TS38.214].For frequency range 1 and 2, if receiver diversity is in use by the UE, the reported SGCS value shall be no lower than the minimum and no higher than the maximum measured values across the receiver branches.For frequency range 1, the reference point for the SGCS shall be the antenna connector of the UE. For frequency range 2, SGCS shall be measured based on the combined signal from antenna elements corresponding to a given receiver branch.
Applicable for RRC_CONNECTED
