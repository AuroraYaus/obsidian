NOTE 1:CSI-RS_RPn is the equivalent power received by an antenna with 0 dBi gain at the centre of the quiet zone configured in the test for the cell n under considerationNOTE 2:δ is the RSRP relative accuracy requirement from Table 10.1.5.2.2-1NOTE 3:Void NOTE 4:X is the Spherical coverage gain difference in dB, derived as (UE Refsens - UE Spherical coverage) from TS 38.101-2 [19] clauses 7.3.2 and 7.3.4, selected according to the UE power class and operating band.
